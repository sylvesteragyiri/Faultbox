See every queue job your app runs

Update the Laravel package (composer update faultbox/laravel) and every job your workers run is reported, successful ones included, with its duration, attempts and how long it waited in the queue. Queued notifications, mailables, listeners and broadcasts are reported under their own class. Runs are sent in batches; payloads and arguments are never sent.

Already updated? Check that FAULTBOX_JOB_RUNS_ENABLED isn't false, then run a job. Failures reported before then are under Failed jobs{{if .Failed.Failures}} ({{compact .Failed.Failures}} in the last 30 days){{end}}.

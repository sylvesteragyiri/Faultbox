Verifying requests

Each request carries X-Faultbox-Timestamp and X-Faultbox-Signature: sha256=…, an HMAC-SHA256 of timestamp + "." + body keyed with the channel secret. Compare it in constant time and reject old timestamps.

Other headers: X-Faultbox-Event (the trigger) and X-Faultbox-Delivery (a unique ID for de-duplicating retries).

How alerts are delivered

  • Alerts are queued separately from ingestion, so slow SMTP or webhook targets never delay storing events.
  • Failed deliveries are retried after 30 s, 2 min, 10 min, 30 min and 2 h, then marked failed. Client errors (4xx) and rejected recipients fail straight away.
  • Every attempt is recorded in the delivery history.
